# Canonical Story Outline

**A Dinner Date with Death** is structured into four narrative acts across 32 major beats.

---

## The Four Principal Beings

- **Death**: A pale, handsome, dark-haired young-looking man wearing elegant black gothic/formal clothing. Not a hooded skeleton. Philosophical, dry, thoughtful, and deeply affected by humanity.
- **Love**: A breathtakingly beautiful woman with long dark curly hair and a shimmering silver/white evening gown. Luminous, defiant, passionate, and an unwavering defender of human mortal life.
- **Fear**: A gaunt, dark-haired gothic figure who acts as Death's chaotic best-friend / cousin-like "bro". Disturbing, funny, unpredictable, irreverent, and supernatural.
- **The General**: An older, imposing aristocratic soldier-philosopher. Grey/silver hair, trimmed beard, ceremonial black military uniform, gold embroidery, medals, and gold epaulettes. Authoritative and uncompromising.

---

## Act I — Death Prepares for New Year's Eve

1. **Mirror Prep**: Death inspects his formal attire and adjusts his tie in front of the ornate mansion mirror.
2. **The Waiting Table**: Examining the banquet table set for two: fine silver, dark mahogany, crimson runner, and vintage wine.
3. **She's Late**: Pacing the quiet mansion hall. Love has not yet arrived.
4. **Spider on the Wall**: A small, quiet observational moment contemplating mortality and patience.
5. **Fear Arrives**: Fear abruptly materializes out of shadow and smoke with chaotic, irreverent energy.
6. **Bad News**: Fear reveals that The General has convened an urgent council regarding the impending mortal century.
7. **Light Up**: Death and Fear share a moment of smoking and cynical camaraderie.
8. **Lift Off**: The mansion fades as Death drifts into memory, recalling how he first encountered Love.

---

## Act II — How Death Met Love

9. **Funeral Vision**: A rain-slicked Victorian cemetery surrounded by weeping mortal mourners.
10. **Silver in the Crowd**: Amidst the sea of dark mourning clothes, a vibrant, glowing silver presence catches Death's gaze.
11. **On the Tombstone**: Death approaches; Love sits perched atop a weathered stone monument, unbothered by death.
12. **The Name's Love**: The two immortals introduce themselves, establishing their natural opposition and instant fascination.
13. **Afterworld Ride**: Traveling together through surreal, cosmic transitional realms between life and eternity.
14. **Back to Earth**: Returning to the mortal realm, recognizing their shared bond.
15. **Dinner Invite**: Death extends an invitation to his mansion for New Year's Eve.
16. **She Arrives**: Memory transitions back to present reality as a knock echoes at the mansion door.

---

## Act III — Dinner Interrupted

17. **Guest in the Mansion**: Love is welcomed inside; the quiet darkness of the mansion is illuminated by her presence.
18. **The Feast**: Sitting together at the prepared table, drinking wine, and sharing centuries of perspective.
19. **Breaking the Ice**: Witty, intimate conversation about humanity's frailty and resilience.
20. **The Tremor**: A sudden violent supernatural vibration shakes the mansion foundation.
21. **Through the Fire**: The hall fireplace flares unnatural azure and gold as uninvited visitors arrive.
22. **The Pitch**: The General enters alongside Fear, demanding an alliance of cosmic forces to subjugate human destiny.
23. **Love Refuses**: Love fiercely rejects The General's authoritarian philosophy, championing human autonomy.
24. **Storm and Broadcast**: A supernatural tempest rages outside as tension reaches a boiling point.

---

## Act IV — Defiance and New Year

25. **The Choice**: Death is pressured to cast the deciding vote between the immortals.
26. **Love Defends Humanity**: Love delivers an impassioned defense of mortals and their capacity for beauty and suffering.
27. **She Walks Away**: Believing Death will side with apathy and order, Love turns toward the door.
28. **Wait**: Death speaks, stopping her in her tracks.
29. **Death Rebukes Fear**: Death reprimands Fear for orchestrating the confrontation and betraying their trust.
30. **Death Rejects the General**: Death stands firmly with Love, repudiating The General's tyrannical crusade.
31. **Out You Go**: The General and Fear are expelled from the mansion into the stormy night.
32. **Happy New Year**: Midnight strikes. Death and Love stand together by the gothic window, watching mortal fireworks light up the dark sky.
